Specialized in automotive test & validation, embedded electrification systems, and system integration. With over 7 years at Valeo — a global Tier 1 automotive supplier — I've contributed to some of the most ambitious EV and hybrid programs in Europe.

My work spans the full V&V lifecycle: from requirements writing and ECU integration to on-vehicle testing and Python automation. I bring both hardware intuition (PCB-level diagnostics, oscilloscopes, DAQ) and software fluency (Python, CAPL, MATLAB) to every project.

Now based in Toronto, Ontario, I'm eager to contribute to Canada's growing electrification and clean-tech ecosystem. Available immediately, open to relocation across Canada.
